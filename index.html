THE PROBLEM

A shelter that suits one region’s conditions may not offer the same thermal comfort in another.

THE APPROACH

Explore a software model around environmental and material parameters, then compare simple temperature profiles to support early design thinking.

THE OUTPUT

The inspected code exposes a material catalog and a 24-hour screening heuristic based on day/night temperatures, wall thickness, conductivity and density.

i

What the current model does not claim: location or weather-data ingestion, humidity, rainfall, wind or solar analysis; whole-building thermal simulation; scientific validation; or construction-ready recommendations. The repository’s own code notes its limits. Orientation, openings, shading and broader comfort analysis remain design directions—not verified capabilities here.
